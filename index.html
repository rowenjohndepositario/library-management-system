Staff / Visitors

People roster

Maintain the private list used to match names or ID numbers at the entrance. No roster is displayed to visitors.

Private information. Upload only names, necessary ID numbers, and program or category. Roster records are not automatically deleted.

Upload a roster spreadsheet

Accepts Excel or CSV with full name, optional ID number, and category. Format ID numbers as text in Excel to preserve leading zeros. Upload separate files for students, faculty, or staff if needed.

Existing people are not deleted or reactivated. Matching IDs may update their category; name-only duplicates are skipped. Use consistent category spelling. Two people with the same name need different IDs for reliable matching.
